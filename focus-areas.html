Focus areas

Where I am sharpening my judgement.

Judgement is the craft; these are the three places I am building it. For each: why it matters, what I have done so far, and what I am working through now.

01

Project and infrastructure finance

Long-lived assets such as water schemes, power plants, industrial parks and PPPs succeed or fail on one question: can their cash flows carry the capital needed to build them? Answering it well, early, decides which projects get built.

What I have done

  • Built a ten-year project finance model for a USD 150 million pharmaceutical industrial park across twelve asset classes, sizing debt to lender coverage ratios and structuring the cash-flow waterfall.
  • Manage the investment pipeline of a blended finance facility that funds early-stage water infrastructure, including national bulk-water, dam and desalination projects brought in through a PPP framework with the national water ministry.
  • Led the exit valuation and structuring analysis for a minority stake in a hydropower project.
  • Rebuilt a facility's five-year financial model to the FAST modelling standard.

What I am working through

What separates a bankable project from a well-intentioned one, and where early preparation money does the most to change the outcome.

02

Credit and capital structuring

Capital a borrower cannot carry is a future write-off, whatever the borrower's size. Structure is how risk gets shared out, and it matters as much for a small enterprise as for a public utility.

What I have done

  • Cross-checked a water utility's investment case against three years of regulator data; the findings put the investment on hold until they were resolved, after which it advanced to Investment Committee due diligence.
  • Maintained the group model behind a USD 12 million programme of structured debt and development finance equity for a seven-subsidiary manufacturing group.
  • Provided lender-appointed oversight of a manufacturer's working capital after a USD 40 million debt restructuring.
  • Carried out buy-side due diligence where the right answer was not to buy, and due diligence on an impact-linked convertible note for a global impact investor.
  • Built financial models for more than ten agribusiness enterprises seeking loans and grants.

What I am working through

How to size and structure capital that a borrower can actually carry when the records are thin and the risks are local.

03

Valuation and exits

Where markets are thin and exits are rare, value has to be argued rather than observed, and the route to an exit often has to be built.

What I have done

  • Valued businesses across agriculture, education, manufacturing and food in Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania, worth over USD 250 million cumulatively.
  • Consolidated and valued a ten-subsidiary healthcare and pharmaceutical group ahead of a partner's exit.
  • Built employee share option valuation models under IFRS 2.
  • Worked on the accountant's report and due diligence for a listing on the Nairobi Securities Exchange.
  • Produced a phased exit valuation in separate versions for an Investment Committee, prospective buyers and management.

What I am working through

What a stake is worth when there is no liquid market, and how to structure an exit before the window closes.

The lens across all three

Judging patient, recyclable capital

My current role adds one question to everything above: how should concessional capital that is meant to come back and be lent again be judged? IRR rewards or punishes timing as much as performance. Multiples on invested capital, how positions are worked out when they go wrong, and measured results tell a fuller story. It is the thread I expect to write about most.
