Pesalink is a national payment rail owned by Kenya's banks through their industry association: shared infrastructure that no single bank would build alone.

I was deployed there full-time by KKCO East Africa to run finance operations.

I managed accounts payable and receivable, monthly payroll for more than fifty staff, and tax compliance (VAT, PAYE and withholding tax) in a central-bank-regulated environment. I coordinated capital expenditure funding with the parent association and supported delivery of the payments infrastructure project led by its technology partner. I introduced an automated card-loading tool, contributed to drafting the internal finance policy and manual, and prepared board materials across five departmental budget lines.

## What I took from it

It was my first view from the inside of how shared infrastructure gets funded, governed and upgraded, and why regulators care about it.
