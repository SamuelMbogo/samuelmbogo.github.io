When a foreign education group enters a new market, the hardest part is not finding a school for sale. It is finding one whose values, community and campus match its own, at a price within its budget.

A large faith-based education group from Southeast Asia wanted to acquire an existing school in Nairobi as its first step into East Africa.

My role was to find and qualify the targets.

I researched Nairobi's private school market, carried out fieldwork, and screened targets against the group's criteria: educational philosophy, community standing, campus and infrastructure, and the ticket size it had set aside. I prepared teasers and proposals, engaged with school owners and prospective partners, and presented leads and quarterly updates to the client. Nairobi's international school segment was active at the time, with several regional and international groups acquiring or expanding campuses.

The deal did not proceed to close.

## What I took from it

In a values-led acquisition, the qualitative screen comes first and does most of the work. Price only matters once the fit is right.
