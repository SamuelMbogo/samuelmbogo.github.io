Agriculture is a large share of Kenya's economy and a small share of its bank lending. Much of that gap is about bankability rather than a shortage of capital.

I built three-statement financial models with scenario and sensitivity analysis, cash flow projections and break-even analyses for more than ten agribusiness clients, and prepared business cases and pitch decks for their loan and grant applications. I also researched the financial partners relevant to the sector, from banks and microfinance lenders to donors and development partners.

## What I took from it

It was where I first asked the question that has shaped my career since: which kind of capital fits which kind of project?
