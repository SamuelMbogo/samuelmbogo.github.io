An NGO funded by several institutional donors has to satisfy more than local law. Each donor brings its own rules, often stricter than the law, and all of them apply to the same policies at once.

An international NGO operating in Kenya found that its finance and HR policies had fallen behind both the law and its donors' requirements.

My role was on the team that closed the gap.

We reviewed the existing policies against Kenyan legislation, the organisation's donor compliance requirements and good practice for international NGOs, including safeguarding and anti-fraud provisions. The gap analysis showed where policies had fallen behind legal changes or lacked the specificity donors require, and the team delivered revised, adoption-ready policies.

## What I took from it

When several rulebooks apply at once, the practical answer is usually the strictest one, written clearly enough that staff can follow it.
