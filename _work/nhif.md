I supported member registration, records management and the counter-checking of inpatient and outpatient insurance claims at one of Nairobi's busiest branches, in the first weeks of the pandemic.

## What I took from it

It was my first view of public financing: pooled money paying for a public good, and how access depends on the process around it.
