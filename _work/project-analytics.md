A large manufacturer generates far more transactions than a traditional audit can sample. Data analytics can test all of them.

One of East Africa's major manufacturers commissioned a special internal audit to find where its operations were losing efficiency.

My role was to support that audit with data analytics.

Instead of testing a sample, the team analysed full data populations for the patterns that point to structural inefficiencies and control gaps rather than one-off errors, and turned what the data showed into findings and recommendations for management.

## What I took from it

Analytics changes the conversation from "we found an error in the sample" to "here is the pattern across the business, and here is what it costs".
