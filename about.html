About

Judgement, built deal by deal.

I'm Samuel Mbogo, an investment analyst at KIFFWA, the Kenya Innovative Finance Facility for Water, a blended finance facility funded primarily by the Embassy of the Kingdom of the Netherlands.

I manage KIFFWA's investment pipeline: the front door through which early-stage water projects, from community schemes to national public-private partnerships, apply for recoverable, below-market development capital. Alongside that I work on credit cross-checks, portfolio monitoring, exits and the facility's next-phase strategy.

Every role so far has moved me closer to one question: which capital makes a project happen? I first saw public financing up close at the National Hospital Insurance Fund. At Acorn, I tracked construction costs on student housing financed through a green bond that a development finance guarantee had lifted above Kenya's sovereign rating. At Smart Farmer I modelled small agribusinesses seeking loans and grants and mapped the development partners behind them, and at KKCO I ran the finance function for a national payments infrastructure provider.

At RSM Eastern Africa the question became technical: lender-grade project finance models, valuations, buy-side due diligence, forensic investigation and restructuring oversight, for clients across seven countries.

I moved to the investing side because I wanted to live with the consequences of the analysis. In advisory you hand the model over. In investing you watch the project draw down, report, sometimes stall, and you help decide what happens next. That is where judgement gets tested, and where I want to keep building it.

I'm still building. The three areas I'm focused on, and the questions I'm working through in each, are on the Focus Areas page.
