About

My path to the deployment side of capital.

Investment Analyst at KIFFWA, working on how concessional and commercial capital actually reaches water service providers in Kenya.

My career has traced a deliberate arc from accounting and finance operations into the structural end of capital markets. I began in claims and accounting internships, learned outsourced finance operations at KKCO where I ran full finance-function support for a fintech client, and moved into transaction advisory at RSM Eastern Africa, where I earned Project Senior responsibilities inside a Semi Senior grade, owning modelling output and client delivery on mandates ranging from a USD 12 million paper-manufacturing debt raise to a USD 150 million pharmaceutical industrial park.

At KIFFWA I now sit on the deployment side of that same capital stack, working on how concessional and commercial funding actually reaches water service providers in Kenya. The work runs from origination and due diligence at one end to structuring, IC advisory, and exit management at the other, alongside institutional-level work on KIFFWA's own next-phase capital strategy.

The through-line across both chapters has been the same: producing analysis that is defensible in front of an Investment Committee, a donor, or a court.

Credentials

BCom Finance from the University of Nairobi (Second Class Honours, Upper Division). CPA(K) with ICPAK (Member No. 32868). Certified Investment and Financial Analyst (CIFA) Advanced Level in progress with ICIFA. Financial Modelling & Valuation Analyst (FMVA) and Business Intelligence & Data Analyst (BIDA) from the Corporate Finance Institute. Fellow of the Africa Impact Investing Fellowship (AIIF).

Where I'm based

Nairobi, Kenya. Working across the East African region, with prior transaction experience extending to Uganda, Tanzania, Mauritius, and Indonesia.
