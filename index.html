The craft

Judgement is the work.

Models, valuations and credit memos are tools. The craft is judgement: deciding what a project can carry, what a stake is really worth, and when the right answer is no. That judgement travels, from a USD 150 million industrial park to a small water enterprise, and it is what I am building, deal by deal.
