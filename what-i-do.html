What I Do

Six areas where I work.

Investment analysis in blended finance sits at the intersection of many disciplines. Here's where I've built depth so far.

01

Debt Capital Structuring & Blended Finance

Structuring concessional, commercial, and blended debt transactions for infrastructure clients. Working knowledge of first-loss tranches, guarantees, subordinated loans, catalytic grants, and layered facility models. Familiar with the DFI Enhanced Principles for the Use of Blended Concessional Finance and their application to co-investment structuring.

02

Origination & Due Diligence

End-to-end Quick Scan triage of inbound project proposals against mandate fit, technical viability, sizing, and SDG alignment. Regulatory and sector cross-checks (WASREB Impact Reports, K-WASH compliance data) used to validate or challenge sponsor-submitted projections at investment intake.

03

Financial Modelling & Valuation

3-statement, project finance, and DCF modelling with scenario, sensitivity, and stress-testing overlays. Valuations using DCF, NAV, and comparable-multiples methods. FAST modelling-standard compliance for fund-level and project-level models. Models built for transactions ranging from USD 12 million debt raises to USD 150 million industrial parks.

04

Transaction Structuring & Exit Management

Exit valuation modelling for portfolio SPV equity stakes, with tailored analytical outputs for Investment Committee, external buyer, and Management Committee audiences. Coordination of legal counsel on exit mechanics including regulatory lock-in provisions, transfer restrictions, and tax structuring.

05

Asset Management & Portfolio Oversight

Portfolio monitoring, investment appraisal, and Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning (MEL) for water infrastructure projects. Portfolio-return metric design (MOIC over IRR for patient-capital contexts), pipeline management, and alignment of capital deployment with fund-level investment objectives.

06

Stakeholder & Institutional Relationship Management

Direct working relationships with development finance institutions, foundations, multilaterals, and technical partners. Meeting facilitation, concept-note development, and formal minute-taking through to countersignature across active partnership tracks.
