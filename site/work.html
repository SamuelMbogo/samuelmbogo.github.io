Selected Work

Five projects that shaped the analyst I am.

Names of counterparties are described at a general level where confidentiality still applies. Cumulative deal value across my prior advisory work exceeds USD 400 million.

Hydropower SPV Exit Valuation

KIFFWA · Lead Analyst · Ongoing

Delivered exit valuation for a 27.5% equity stake in a portfolio hydropower project, iterating through three model versions and producing a final simplified workbook with three tailored analytical write-ups for the Investment Committee, prospective external buyers, and the Management Committee. Coordinating with external legal counsel on PPA-triggered lock-in mechanics, transfer routing, and tax structuring.

Utility Creditworthiness Cross-Check

KIFFWA · Due Diligence Analyst · 2026

Cross-checked a Kenyan water utility applicant's Quick Scan projections against three consecutive WASREB Impact Reports, surfacing a 15.8-point year-on-year fall in the Creditworthiness Index, declining O&M cost coverage, and metering and collection ratios materially below the applicant's base-case model assumptions. Led to a hold-and-clarify recommendation ahead of Investment Committee.

KIFFWA III Strategic Plan & Model Rebuild

KIFFWA · Contributor · 2026

Contributed to KIFFWA's next-phase strategic plan for 2027 to 2031, reviewing four draft strategy documents and a financial projections workbook, and producing the polished 58-page final document for presentation to the facility's anchor funder. Rebuilt the underlying fund-level financial model to FAST modelling standards, surfacing cashflow constraints that the prior version had obscured.

Project Cure — Pharmaceutical Industrial Park

RSM · Project Finance Analyst · Kenya

Developed a 10-year, 3-statement project finance model covering 12 distinct asset classes for a USD 150 million pharmaceutical industrial park, including vaccine fill-and-finish, sterile and non-sterile pharmaceuticals, diagnostics, contract manufacturing, and shared services. Formulated per-asset-class assumptions against industry benchmarks and stress-tested for cost overruns and revenue delays.

Project Stationer — Regional Paper Manufacturing Group

RSM · Lead Financial Modeller · Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Mauritius

Conducted in-depth analysis of consolidated financials for the parent and seven subsidiaries over a five-year horizon. Designed and built the financial model with stress-testing across multiple scenarios that supported a successful USD 12 million debt raise for the group's expansion strategy.
