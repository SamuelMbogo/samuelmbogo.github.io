Services

Advisory work I take on outside my primary role.

Selective advisory work in areas where I have depth. All engagements are handled with attention to conflict-of-interest and confidentiality with my primary employer.

Financial Modelling & Feasibility Studies

Custom-built financial models for fundraising, feasibility assessment, and strategic planning. 3-statement models, project finance models, DCF valuations, and stress-tested scenarios. Sector-agnostic, with deep expertise in infrastructure, agribusiness, and manufacturing. FAST modelling-standard compliance where required.

Business Valuation

Independent valuations for M&A, shareholder disputes, regulatory compliance, and strategic decision-making. DCF, NAV, and comparable-multiples methods. Prior valuation work has covered transactions cumulatively exceeding USD 250 million across agriculture, education, manufacturing, and food sectors.

Deal Advisory

End-to-end support for capital raising, investor engagement, and transaction structuring. Teasers, information memoranda, investor targeting, negotiation support, and investment committee preparation. Experience across debt, equity, and blended finance structures.

Restructuring & Turnaround Advisory

Financial restructuring support for distressed companies, including asset tracing, creditor negotiations, and court-mandated Statement of Affairs reporting. Prior experience supporting court-administered turnarounds through to successful recovery.

Project Finance Advisory

Structuring and advisory for infrastructure and development projects, including blended finance and impact-investment instruments. Financial feasibility studies, capital structure design, and stakeholder engagement for large-scale infrastructure projects.
